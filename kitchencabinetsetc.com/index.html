Overview & Strategy Approach

This SEO strategy positions Kitchen Cabinets Etc. as the premier cabinet and countertop showroom destination on the Eastside of King County, Washington. With over 30 years in business, the client operates a full-service showroom in Bellevue offering kitchen cabinets, bathroom cabinets, and countertops. The strategy focuses on 30 high-value keyword-city combinations across 6 carefully selected Eastside markets, targeting affluent homeowners who are actively searching for a showroom to visit. Every keyword was selected to accurately reflect the client's retail showroom business model and to cover a distinct, high-intent service line. Mercer Island has been added as a high-priority market at the client's direction, reflecting the area's strong buying power and the client's consistent business there. The broader Seattle market has been intentionally excluded per client instruction.

Market Analysis & Tiering

We analyzed all major communities in the Eastside King County corridor and evaluated each market based on population size, proximity to the Bellevue showroom, household income levels, and homeownership rates. The 6 markets selected for Plan B include the 5 largest Tier 1 communities (Bellevue, Renton, Kirkland, Redmond, and Sammamish) plus Mercer Island, which has been added as a high-priority market at the client's direction. While Mercer Island's population is smaller, the client consistently does meaningful business there and the area's exceptional buying power makes it a strategic priority. Keyword Analysis & Tiering

We researched 17 relevant keywords across two core service lines for Kitchen Cabinets Etc. and organized them into tight, thematic buckets based on a strict core-term rule: a keyword is only a variant within a bucket if it shares the exact same core term. For example, "bathroom vanity" and "bathroom cabinets" are two separate buckets because they represent different products, even though both are bathroom-related. This bucketing discipline ensures that the selected keywords are truly distinct and non-overlapping, and that the strategy covers as many of the client's service lines as possible. All search volumes listed are national monthly averages used as a relative indicator of demand.
