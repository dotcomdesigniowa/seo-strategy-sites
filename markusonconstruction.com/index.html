Overview & Strategy Approach

This document outlines a comprehensive, data-driven SEO strategy designed specifically for Markuson Construction Inc. The primary goal is to increase online visibility, drive qualified traffic, and generate consistent leads within key service areas across the Omaha metro area and Council Bluffs, Iowa. The strategy is built on a foundation of high-intent, commercially relevant keywords mapped to high-value geographic markets; rather than targeting a broad, unfocused audience, this plan surgically places Markuson Construction in front of homeowners and property managers who are actively searching for concrete contractors, masonry services, chimney repair, tuckpointing, and retaining wall work right now. Every keyword combination was selected based on national search volume data from SEMrush, commercial intent signals, and strategic market opportunity within the bi-state Omaha metro, with the result being a plan that maximizes the value of every dollar invested.

Market Analysis & Tiering

We analyzed all cities and communities within the Omaha metro service area, spanning Douglas County, NE, Sarpy County, NE, Washington County, NE, and Pottawattamie County, IA, and evaluated each market based on population size, proximity to Markuson Construction's Omaha headquarters, and overall search opportunity. The analysis produced three tiers of market priority. Tier 1 markets represent the highest-population, highest-opportunity targets and form the backbone of the strategy. Tier 2 markets are strong secondary communities that are ideal candidates for plan expansion. Tier 3 markets are smaller communities that become viable as domain authority grows over time.
