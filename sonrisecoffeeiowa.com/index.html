Overview & Strategy Approach

This document outlines a comprehensive, data-driven SEO strategy designed specifically for SonRise Coffee. The primary goal is to increase online visibility, drive qualified foot traffic, and generate consistent event bookings across SonRise Coffee's service region centered on Riverside, Iowa and extending into the Iowa City metro. The strategy is built on a foundation of high-intent, commercially relevant keywords mapped to high-value geographic markets. Rather than targeting a broad, unfocused audience, this plan surgically places SonRise Coffee in front of consumers who are actively searching for a coffee shop, cafe, ice cream shop, or event venue in the Iowa City region right now. Every keyword combination was selected based on national search volume data, commercial intent signals, and strategic market opportunity, with the result being a plan that maximizes the value of every dollar invested at $2,000 per month.

Market Analysis & Tiering

We analyzed cities and communities across SonRise Coffee's service region and evaluated each market based on population size, proximity to the Riverside, IA headquarters, and overall search opportunity. The analysis produced three tiers of market priority. Tier 1 markets (Iowa City, Coralville, North Liberty) represent the highest-population, highest-opportunity targets and anchor the strategy's event venue reach. Tier 2 markets (Washington) represent strong mid-size communities with meaningful search volume. Tier 3 markets (Solon, Kalona, Sigourney, Wellman, Lone Tree, Riverside, Keota, Hills) are the small communities that form SonRise Coffee's core local audience for coffee, ice cream, and cafe services. The strategy balances broad event venue reach into Iowa City with deep local presence in the surrounding communities.
