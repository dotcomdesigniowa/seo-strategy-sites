Overview & Strategy Approach

Discount Tile Outlet is a family-owned tile showroom in Bellevue, Washington, that has served the King County area with distinction since 2010. For over fifteen years, the company has built a reputation for offering a vast selection of high-quality tile at affordable prices, supported by a team of experienced design specialists. This initial SEO strategy focuses on capturing high-intent customers searching for tile stores and showrooms in the affluent Eastside communities surrounding the Bellevue headquarters. The plan is designed to establish a strong digital footprint in these core markets, driving qualified foot traffic to the showroom from customers who are actively looking to purchase tile for their home renovation projects. By targeting three distinct keyword categories, this strategy positions Discount Tile Outlet to connect with a wide range of customers, from those with a specific product in mind to those just beginning their design journey.

02

Market Analysis & Tiering

The service area for this strategy is the high-income corridor of Eastside King County, a region known for its robust housing market and significant investment in home renovations. The plan specifically excludes Seattle, per client instruction, to concentrate resources on the communities most likely to visit a Bellevue showroom. The eight targeted markets are divided into two tiers based on population and strategic value. Tier 1 includes the largest and most affluent Eastside cities: Bellevue, Kirkland, Redmond, Sammamish, Issaquah, and Bothell. These areas represent the core of the Eastside's residential and economic activity. Tier 2 consists of Mercer Island and Woodinville, smaller but extremely affluent communities with a high concentration of luxury homes and a strong demand for premium design materials.

Keyword Analysis & Tiering

We researched 20 relevant keywords for Discount Tile Outlet and grouped them into three strategic tiers based on customer intent and search behavior. All search volumes listed are national monthly averages and are used as a relative indicator of demand to guide prioritization, not as projections of local traffic. The keyword strategy is built around three distinct customer mindsets: Destination Shoppers using broad terms like "tile store," Ready-to-Buy Shoppers using specific terms like "tile flooring near me," and Design-Focused Shoppers using terms like "tile showroom near me." Keywords containing "near me" are shown here for reference but are treated as equivalent to their base keyword in the final matrix, since the city name serves the same geographic function when appended.

Final Keyword-City Strategy Matrix

This is the core deliverable of the Plan A strategy. The matrix below maps 3 high-value keywords across 8 priority markets for a total of 20 targeted keyword-city combinations. Each combination represents a unique page or content target designed to rank Discount Tile Outlet for that specific search in that specific city. The "tile store" keyword is deployed across all eight markets to capture the widest possible audience of local shoppers. The more specific "tile flooring" keyword is focused on the seven largest markets, while the design-oriented "tile showroom" keyword is concentrated on the six most affluent communities where design services are in highest demand.

05

Keywords Not Used & Rationale

The following keywords were identified during research but were not included in the current 20-combination plan. These are not discarded; they represent a clear, prioritized roadmap for future plan expansion. Broad, high-volume keywords like "tile flooring" and "porcelain tile" were avoided because they are dominated by national manufacturers and big-box retailers. Redundant terms such as "tile shop near me" were consolidated under the primary "tile store" keyword to avoid diluting SEO efforts. Finally, highly specific or niche terms and commercially-focused terms like "tile supply near me" were set aside to maintain a clear focus on the core retail customer.

06

Additional Opportunities

As Discount Tile Outlet's digital presence grows, there are several clear paths for expanding this initial SEO strategy. The Level B plan introduces more specific product keywords like "porcelain tile store" and expands existing keywords into the growing communities of Renton and Kenmore. The Level C plan targets the contractor and trade professional audience by adding commercially-focused keywords. The Level D plan proposes a significant expansion into the broader natural stone and mosaic tile categories, while also extending geographic reach into southern King County markets.
