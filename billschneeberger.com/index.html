Market Analysis & Tiering

We analyzed cities and communities within Lake County and northern Cook County, evaluating each market based on population size, proximity to Bill Schneeberger Painting & Decorating's Northbrook headquarters, residential density, and the affluence level of the homeowner base. The North Shore of Chicago is one of the most desirable residential markets in the country, and the five communities selected for this plan represent the highest-opportunity targets within the client's natural service radius. The analysis produced three tiers of market priority. Tier 1 markets represent the highest-population, highest-opportunity targets and form the backbone of the strategy. Tier 2 markets are strong secondary communities that are ideal candidates for plan expansion.
