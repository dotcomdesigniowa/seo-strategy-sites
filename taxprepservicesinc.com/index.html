Overview & Strategy Approach

This document outlines a comprehensive, data-driven SEO strategy designed specifically for Tax Prep Services Inc.. The primary goal is to increase online visibility, drive qualified traffic, and generate consistent leads across the Inland Empire region of Southern California. The strategy is built on a foundation of high-intent, commercially relevant keywords mapped to the highest-value geographic markets across Riverside County and San Bernardino County. Every keyword was selected based on national search volume data, commercial intent signals, and the client's stated focus on Tax Services, Tax Preparation, and Tax Planning. Rather than targeting a broad, unfocused audience, this plan surgically places Tax Prep Services Inc. in front of individuals and business owners who are actively searching for a local tax professional right now.
