Overview & Strategy Approach

This SEO strategy positions Vanities Etc as the premier bathroom vanity and cabinet showroom destination on the Eastside of King County, Washington. The client operates a full-service showroom in Bellevue offering bathroom vanities, bathroom cabinets, stone and quartz countertops, and decorative hardware. The SEO Booster plan focuses on 10 high-value keyword-city combinations: 2 carefully selected keywords deployed across the 5 largest Eastside markets. Every keyword was chosen to accurately reflect the client's bathroom showroom identity and to cover a distinct, high-intent service line. The Seattle market has been intentionally excluded per client instruction, keeping the campaign tightly focused on the Eastside corridor where the showroom's customer base is concentrated.

02

Market Analysis & Tiering

We analyzed all major communities in the Eastside King County corridor and evaluated each market based on population size, proximity to the Bellevue showroom, household income levels, and homeownership rates. Markets are selected in strict top-down order by population within each tier. The 5 markets selected for the SEO Booster plan are the 5 largest Tier 1 communities in the region: Bellevue, Renton, Kirkland, Redmond, and Sammamish. Seattle has been excluded from this strategy per client instruction. Mercer Island, for which the client already has an existing city page, is noted as an existing asset but falls outside the top 5 by population and is not included in the current plan's matrix.

Keyword Analysis & Tiering

We researched 35 relevant keywords across seven service-line families for Vanities Etc and organized them into tight, thematic buckets based on a strict core-term rule: a keyword is only a variant within a bucket if it shares the exact same core term. Different core terms always form separate buckets, regardless of how related the products may be. For example, "bathroom vanity" and "bathroom cabinets" are separate families, "bathroom countertops" is its own family, and "cabinet hardware" is its own family. Two keywords were selected for the SEO Booster plan, one from each of the two highest-priority service lines.

Keywords Not Used & Rationale

The following keywords were identified during research but were not included in the current 10-combination SEO Booster plan.
