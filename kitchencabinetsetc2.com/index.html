Overview & Strategy Approach

This SEO strategy positions Kitchen Cabinets Etc 2 as the premier cabinet design showroom in the Phoenix East Valley and surrounding Pinal County communities. Headquartered in Gold Canyon, AZ, the client offers kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities, bathroom cabinets, custom closets, laundry room cabinets, and countertops. The strategy focuses on 20 high-value keyword-city combinations across the 5 largest markets within the client's service radius, targeting homeowners who are actively searching for a showroom to visit. Every keyword was selected to accurately reflect the client's design showroom business model and to cover a distinct, high-intent service line. The 4 selected keywords represent 4 distinct service lines: kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities, bathroom cabinets, and custom closets.

Market Analysis & Tiering

We analyzed all major communities within the client's service radius across both Pinal County and the Phoenix East Valley (Maricopa County). Markets are selected in strict top-down order by population within each tier. The 5 markets selected for Plan A are the 5 largest communities within a practical 30-mile service radius: Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Queen Creek, and Apache Junction. Keyword Analysis & Tiering

We researched 57 relevant keywords across six service lines for Kitchen Cabinets Etc 2 and organized them into tight, thematic buckets based on a strict core-term rule: a keyword is only a variant within a bucket if it shares the exact same core term. Different words mean different buckets, without exception. Kitchen cabinets and outdoor kitchen are separate buckets. Bathroom vanity and bathroom cabinets are separate buckets. Granite countertops and quartz countertops are each their own separate buckets. This approach ensures that no keyword is misrepresented as a variant of a service line it does not belong to. The 4 selected keywords each represent a distinct, high-volume service line that Kitchen Cabinets Etc 2 explicitly offers.
