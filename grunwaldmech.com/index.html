Overview & Strategy Approach

This document outlines a comprehensive, data-driven SEO strategy designed specifically for Grunwald Mechanical Contractors & Engineers, a family-owned plumbing, heating, and cooling company serving the greater Omaha metro area since 1899. The strategy also encompasses services offered under the Larry's Boiler Service brand, which operates under the same ownership and serves the same geographic footprint. The primary goal is to increase online visibility, drive qualified leads, and establish Grunwald Mechanical as the dominant digital presence for plumbing and HVAC services across the nine-county service region spanning eastern Nebraska and western Iowa. Every keyword combination was selected based on national search volume data, commercial intent signals, and strategic market opportunity within the defined service counties, with the result being a plan that maximizes the value of every dollar invested across both residential and commercial service lines.

Market Analysis & Tiering

We analyzed all cities and communities within the nine-county service region (Douglas, Washington, Dodge, Saunders, Sarpy, and Lancaster counties in Nebraska; Pottawattamie, Harrison, and Mills counties in Iowa) and evaluated each market based on population size, proximity to Grunwald Mechanical's Omaha headquarters, and overall search opportunity. The analysis produced three tiers of market priority. Tier 1 markets represent the highest-population, highest-opportunity targets and form the backbone of the strategy. Tier 2 markets are strong secondary communities that are ideal candidates for plan expansion. Tier 3 markets are smaller communities that become viable as domain authority grows over time.
