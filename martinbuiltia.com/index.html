Overview & Strategy Approach

This document outlines a comprehensive, data-driven SEO strategy designed specifically for Martin Built Homes. The primary goal is to increase online visibility, drive qualified traffic, and generate consistent leads across the Cedar Rapids and Marion corridor — the two markets where Martin Built Homes does most of its construction. The strategy is built around what the business actually delivers: custom home construction, new home building, residential construction, floor plan customization, and home design. Every keyword combination was selected based on national search volume data, commercial intent signals, and real proof of local delivery. Market weighting reflects where Martin Built Homes actually builds — Cedar Rapids and Marion receive the deepest coverage — with Iowa City, Coralville, Hiawatha, and Mount Vernon included as secondary and client-specified targets. A critical implementation note: several existing thin pages on the current website target the same keyword-city combinations as this strategy. Before or alongside launch, each overlapping page should be either rewritten and expanded into the new target URL, or 301-redirected to the new page. Publishing new pages alongside old thin pages on a low-authority domain will cause keyword cannibalization and slow ranking progress significantly.

Market Analysis & Tiering

We analyzed all cities and communities within the Iowa Corridor region spanning Linn and Johnson Counties. Each market was evaluated based on population size, proximity to Martin Built Homes' Cedar Rapids headquarters, and overall search opportunity. The analysis produced three tiers of market priority. Tier 1 markets represent the highest-population, highest-opportunity targets and form the backbone of the strategy. Tier 2 markets are strong secondary communities ideal for plan expansion. Tier 3 markets are smaller communities specifically requested by Martin Built Homes that carry strong custom home building potential.

Final Keyword-City Strategy Matrix

This is the core deliverable of the Level A plan. The matrix below maps 7 high-value keywords across 6 priority markets for a total of 20 targeted keyword-city combinations. Each combination represents a unique page or content target designed to rank Martin Built Homes for that specific search in that specific city. Market weight reflects where Martin Built Homes actually builds: Cedar Rapids (HQ) and Marion receive the deepest keyword coverage, followed by Iowa City as a secondary market, and Coralville, Hiawatha, and Mount Vernon as targeted outlier markets. "Near me" variants have been removed from the matrix, as the city name appended to each keyword serves the same geographic function. Implementation note: For each combination that overlaps an existing page on the current website, rewrite and expand the existing page into the new target rather than publishing a second URL, or 301-redirect the old page to the new one. Duplicate thin pages on a low-authority domain will cause keyword cannibalization and delay rankings.
