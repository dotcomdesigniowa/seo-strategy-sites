Market Analysis & Tiering

ABC Bilco's service area encompasses one of the most densely populated and economically significant regions in the world: the New York City metropolitan area. The strategy divides this vast territory into logical, tiered markets to ensure coverage is allocated effectively. Tier 1 markets represent the core of the service area, including all five NYC boroughs and the combined Long Island (Nassau and Suffolk counties) market. These areas contain an immense concentration of both residential and commercial properties, representing the largest and most immediate opportunity for lead generation. Tier 2 markets consist of major suburban and secondary urban hubs that are critical to regional commerce, such as Westchester County and key cities in Northern New Jersey. By strategically allocating keywords across these tiers, the plan ensures that ABC Bilco achieves visibility in the markets most likely to produce qualified, high-value customers.

Keyword Analysis & Tiering

The keyword selection is built on a data-driven understanding of how customers search for access product installation and remodeling services in the NYC region. Search volume for generic terms like "basement door installation" is negligible in the US market. The overwhelming majority of access product search intent is captured by the Bilco brand name, with terms like "bilco door" and "bilco doors" generating over 17,000 monthly searches nationally. Our strategy capitalizes on this by prioritizing the low-competition, high-intent keyword "bilco door installer" (590 monthly searches, Difficulty: 2/100). To capture hyper-local search intent, the strategy includes "cellar door installation" (210 monthly searches, Difficulty: 3/100), which is the dominant regional term in the Northeast. "Roof hatch installation" (110 monthly searches, Difficulty: 6/100) addresses the commercial segment, targeting building owners and property managers in key commercial hubs. Finally, "bathroom remodeling contractor" (49,500 monthly searches) is introduced as the fourth keyword to establish visibility for ABC Bilco's remodeling division in the two highest-density markets. All search volumes listed are national monthly averages and are used as a relative indicator of demand to guide prioritization.
