Overview & Strategy Approach

This document outlines a comprehensive, data-driven SEO strategy designed specifically for Sure Shot Billiards & Darts. The primary goal is to increase online visibility, drive qualified traffic, and generate consistent leads and foot traffic within key markets across the Tri-Cities region and surrounding areas of Washington, Oregon, and Idaho. Every keyword combination was selected based on national search volume data, commercial intent signals, and strategic market opportunity within the 120-mile service radius, with the result being a plan that maximizes the value of every dollar invested.

Market Analysis & Tiering

We analyzed all major cities and communities within a 120-mile radius of Sure Shot Billiards & Darts' location on N Columbia Center Blvd in Kennewick, Washington. This radius encompasses the Tri-Cities core (Kennewick, Richland, and Pasco), as well as major regional hubs in Washington, Oregon, and Idaho. Each market was evaluated based on population size, proximity to the store, and overall search opportunity. The analysis produced three tiers of market priority. Tier 1 markets represent the highest-population, highest-opportunity targets and form the backbone of the strategy. Tier 2 markets are strong secondary communities that are ideal candidates for plan expansion.

Keyword Analysis & Tiering

We researched 74 relevant keywords for Sure Shot Billiards & Darts and grouped them into four strategic tiers based on service type, local intent, and competitive opportunity. All search volumes listed are national monthly averages and are used as a relative indicator of demand to guide prioritization, not as projections of local traffic. The four selected keywords are all service-oriented with strong local intent — categories where a local specialist can realistically compete and win on Page 1. Generic product terms dominated by national retailers with heavy Product Listing Ad spend are excluded from the matrix and documented in the Not Used section. Keywords containing "near me" are shown here for reference but are treated as equivalent to their base keyword in the final matrix, since the city name serves the same geographic function when appended.
