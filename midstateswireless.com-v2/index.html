Overview & Strategy Approach

This document outlines a second, alternative SEO strategy for Mid States Wireless — a nationwide, keyword-first approach that targets high-intent search terms without geographic modifiers. Rather than pairing keywords with specific cities and states, this strategy builds national organic presence for 30 standalone keywords across 12 service line categories. The goal is to rank Mid States Wireless for the terms that buyers search regardless of location: "two way radio dealer," "security camera installation," "managed IT services," "access control system," and more. This approach is particularly powerful for a company that serves B2B and government clients who may search nationally before selecting a regional vendor. Every keyword was selected based on national search volume, commercial intent, and alignment with Mid States Wireless's core service offerings across communications, security, and IT.
